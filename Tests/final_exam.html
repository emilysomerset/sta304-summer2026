Coverage

You are strongly encouraged to review the following materials carefully before the test. These are roughly listed in order of importance. There are two practice exams available that you could use to test your knowledge.

  • Exam information page
  • All lecture materials from the entire course
  • Exercises and examples from lecture slides
  • Learning activities. Note that R code from these activities will not be covered. However, their underlying concepts, which are also covered in class, can be included. 
  • The suggested textbook problems.
  • Multiple choice questions posted for test 1 and test 2.
  • Term test 1 and 2. Especially if you missed a test, it would be good to try this as if you were taking the exam. Review the material that you think you could improve on based on your performance. Check your answers with the solutions provided.
  • Practice problems (no solutions provided) Note: There are ALOT of practice problems here, some may be more useful to you than others. I would focus on the ones that you find most challenging then do the rest if you have time.
  • Practice final exam 1 (no solutions provided)
  • Practice final exam 2 (no solutions provided)
