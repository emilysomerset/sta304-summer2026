Learning Outcomes
  • Distinguish between observational studies and randomized experiments and explain the types of research questions each can answer.
  • Explain the concept of a causal effect and describe why randomized experiments can be used to investigate causal relationships.
  • Describe the missing data mechanisms MCAR, MAR, and NMAR, and explain how they differ.
  • Evaluate the potential impact of nonresponse and missing data on survey estimates.
  • Describe common strategies for reducing nonresponse in surveys.
  • Explain the rationale behind the two-stage approach to nonresponse adjustment and describe how it can reduce nonresponse bias.
  • Construct estimators under a two-stage nonresponse design.
